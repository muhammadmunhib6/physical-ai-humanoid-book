Welcome to the **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics** textbook.

- Physical AI & Embodied Intelligence
- ROS 2 for humanoid robots
- Digital twins using Gazebo
- NVIDIA Isaac robotics platform

- Conversational humanoid robots
